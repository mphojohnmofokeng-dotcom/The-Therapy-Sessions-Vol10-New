Welcome

What happens after the conversation?

Sometimes the conversation ends, but the feeling doesn't. There are thoughts still moving, questions still sitting, and things we haven't quite found the words for.

This session lives in that space.

"Same conversations. Different versions. A better us."

The Therapy Sessions Vol. 10 is a collection of New Age RnB, Soul, Alternative RnB, Jazz and Hip Hop influenced sounds. Each record carries its own story. Together, they create another conversation.

The Session

25 records.
25 stories.

Don't just listen to the music. Explore where it came from, who made it, and what was happening around it.
